## Technical Specifications

### Panel Technologies
- Monocrystalline: 15-22% efficiency, 30-40 year lifespan
- Polycrystalline: 15-20% efficiency, 25-30 year lifespan
- Thin-Film: 10-12% efficiency, 20-25 year lifespan

### Inverter Technologies
- String: 95-98% efficiency, R11,250-R18,750
- Micro: 95%+ efficiency, R22,500-R45,000
- Hybrid: 95-98% efficiency, R30,000-R45,000+

### Battery Technologies
- Lithium-Ion: 85-97.5% efficiency, 10+ years, 70% market share
- Lead-Acid: 80-85% efficiency, 3-5 years, declining market
- Flow: 70-85% efficiency, 30 years, emerging utility-scale

### System Configurations
- Grid-Tied: Lowest cost, net metering
- Off-Grid: Complete independence, battery storage
- Hybrid: Best of both, 25% adoption surge
